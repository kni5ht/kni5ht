Abstract

LightEchoNet is a lightweight audio classification framework that satisfies the most stringent edge‑device constraints (<128 KB, <30 MMACs) while remaining competitive with models orders of magnitude larger. The student architecture combines depthwise separable convolutions, residual connections, residual normalization, and attentive global average pooling for efficient and robust feature extraction. To close the performance gap under extreme compression, we propose a two‑stage knowledge distillation strategy that transfers complementary knowledge from a heterogeneous teacher ensemble, including a CLAP‑based multimodal model with a lightweight audio encoder, thereby bringing semantic supervision to the tiny‑model regime. Intermediate feature matching with Huber loss and logit‑level distillation enables effective optimization.

LightEchoNet achieves 60.0% accuracy on TAU Urban Acoustic Scenes 2022 Mobile, 92.0% on ESC‑50, 58.7% mAP on FSD50K, and 97.4% on UrbanSound8K, using 350× fewer parameters than large‑scale pretrained models.

Architecture Components

Efficient design for extreme edge constraints (<128KB, <30MMACs)

  • Depthwise Separable Convolutions (DSC)
    Factorizes standard convolutions into depthwise + pointwise, reducing parameters by 8‑9×
  • Residual Connections
    Mitigates vanishing gradient, enables deeper networks with identity mappings
  • Residual Normalization (ResNorm)
    λ·x + IN(x) – style reset module that attenuates device‑specific variations
  • Attentive Global Average Pooling (AttnGAP)
    Learns attention weights to highlight salient acoustic events before pooling
  • Hybrid BN + ResNorm Strategy
    BN in early/late layers for training stability, ResNorm after each stage for device invariance

Distillation Pipeline

Complementary knowledge transfer from heterogeneous teachers

  • Stage 1 — Inner Teacher
    CNN-based teacher (CP-Mobile) with Huber feature matching (Eq. 5-6) and temperature-scaled KL divergence
    α=0.6, β=0.8, T=5.0 • 50+150 epochs
  • Stage 2 — Outer Teacher Ensemble
    Heterogeneous ensemble of CP-Mobile (CNN), HTS-AT (Transformer), and CLAP+CpMobile (Multimodal)
    α=0.95, T=8.0 • +300 epochs
  • Ensemble Strategy
    Weighted averaging of teacher predictions with CLAP-based multimodal teacher providing semantic supervision via audio-text alignment
  • Training Dynamics
    Progressive refinement: inner teacher establishes strong baseline, outer ensemble adds complementary knowledge for final performance boost
